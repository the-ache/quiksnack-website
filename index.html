Questions, answered

What's the difference between Option 1 and Option 2?

With Option 1, the people using the machine pay per item at the machine. With Option 2, your business covers the snacks as a company benefit and we send one monthly invoice. Either way, you choose the snacks and we supply, deliver, and restock the machine.

Who chooses what goes in the machine?

You do. Pick healthy options, indulgent treats, drinks, or a mix.

How do you know when to restock?

QuikSnack machines are remotely monitored, so we can see stock levels and refill before you run out.

What payment methods do the machines take?

Apple Pay, Google Pay, credit cards, cash, and coins.

What areas do you serve?

We currently serve Huntsville and Madison, Alabama.
